**auto_plant** – plant new seeds straight onto a random empty tile instead of putting them in the Seed Bag. When the farm is full, seeds still go to the bag. Also in **Anki Farm → Settings…**.

**sound** – play retro sound effects in the farm window. Also in **Anki Farm → Settings…**.

**min_answer_seconds** – seeds are only earned when an answer completes a card (it ends up in the review queue; learning steps earn nothing). Answers faster than this don't earn a seed, so you can't farm seeds by clicking through cards.

**show_tooltips** – show a short "🌱 +1 seed" message after each rewarded review.

**show_almanac_on_deck_list** – show the Almanac panel under your decks.

**almanac_collapsed** – remembers whether you folded the Almanac panel. Set automatically.

**seen_howto** – whether the "How to play" page has been shown. Set automatically; set it back to `false` to see it again on the next farm open.

All settings except the last two can also be changed from the **Anki Farm → Settings…** menu in the menu bar.
